What these figures do not say

Excursions are sampled once per decision cycle, not from ticks. Both the best and the worst unrealised marks are read at the close of a cycle, so a swing that happened and reversed between two samples is invisible. The capture ratio above is a floor, not a measurement.

A thin sample is noise. Below about twenty closed trades, expectancy and profit factor are arithmetic rather than evidence. The figures are shown anyway, and labelled, because hiding them invites the guess that they are better than they are.

None of this establishes an edge. The strategies in the repository are written so they can be shown to be wrong, not because they have been shown to be right. In the Alpha Arena competition every frontier model finished underwater, and the one that traded most lost most. Trade frequency is treated here as a risk parameter, not a performance one.

This is a demonstration. Every trade, metric and chart on this site is synthetic, generated by scripts/generate_demo_data.py and committed as a fixture. Nothing here reads a broker, a journal, an account or an API.

Mudhorn Capital is a private vehicle. It trades one paper account, for one person, and takes no money from anyone else. Not an offer, not advice, and not managing anyone else's money.
